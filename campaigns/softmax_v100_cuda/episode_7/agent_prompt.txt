## Mathematical Semantics

Stable row-wise Softmax. For each row i in [0, rows):
  m = max_j(x[i][j])
  s = sum_j(exp(x[i][j] - m))
  y[i][j] = exp(x[i][j] - m) / s

Numerical stability: always subtract row maximum before exponentiation.
Never compute: exp(x) / sum(exp(x)) without subtracting max.
